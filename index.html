About Pasdaran-e-Insaniyat

Built by ordinary people who chose to care.

We are a close-knit team of around 7 to 12 active members and volunteers. We are new, our resources are limited, and we do not pretend otherwise. Our strength is staying close to the people we serve and careful with every rupee trusted to us.

Impact is not only about how much you have — it is also about how faithfully you use what people trust you with.

What guides us

Humanity with accountability

  • Respect: Help should protect human dignity.
  • Transparency: Supporters deserve clarity about funds.
  • Action: We focus on manageable, immediate needs.
  • Community: Everyone can contribute according to capacity.
